Who We Are

Designers who build with their hands.

2NOMADS is a transportation design studio based in Pune, founded by R Donshong Koren — automobile designer, clay modeller and educator, and former Head of the Transportation Design Department in Canada.

We work with OEMs on clay models, design correction and full working prototypes, while our Academy trains the next generation of designers in the same studio, on the same real projects.

Clay Modelling

The craft at the heart of automotive design. Learn tools, armatures, surfacing and how to read highlights — taught by a clay modeller who has trained students under SIAM and MITID.

  • —Traditional clay tools

Digital
Sculpting

Sculpt form on screen with the same eye you use in clay. Build, refine and visualise designs digitally, ready for milling, printing or presentation.

  • —Surface refinement

Scanning

Bridge the physical and digital. Capture clay models and parts in 3D, clean the data and bring it back into the design loop.

  • —3D scanning workflow
  • —Point cloud & mesh cleanup
  • —Clay-to-digital loop
  • —Reverse engineering basics
