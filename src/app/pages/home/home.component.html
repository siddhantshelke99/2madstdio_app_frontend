Who We Are

Designers who build with their hands.

2NOMADS is a transportation design studio based in Pune, founded by R Donshong Koren — automobile designer, clay modeller and educator, and former Head of the Transportation Design Department in Canada.

We work with OEMs on clay models, design correction and full working prototypes, while our Academy trains the next generation of designers in the same studio, on the same real projects.
